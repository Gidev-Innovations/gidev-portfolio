## Sourcing brief (TODO(human))

The set is thin and partly stock. In priority order:

1. **Screenshots of shipped work** – R-Flex on a phone and on desktop; any
   dashboard or checkout you can show. These beat any stock photo.
2. **A short shoot in Nairobi** – the team at work, a client in their shop or
   office using something you built, a phone showing an M-Pesa STK prompt on
   your checkout. Real people, daylight, no posed handshakes.
3. **Stock, if needed** – Africa-first libraries (e.g. Nappy, Pexels/Unsplash
   collections by African photographers). Record photographer and licence in
   the table above. Avoid recognisable foreign landmarks and branded cups.

Wanted shapes: one 16:9 hero (≥1600px wide), three 16:10 service images, one
portrait (4:5) per project for device mock-ups.
